VidLimit Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026. Contact: support@vidlimit.com

VidLimit is a Mac app that compresses a video so it fits under a size limit you choose. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your data. The short version: your videos stay on your Mac, and VidLimit collects nothing about you.

Your videos

  • Compression runs entirely on your Mac and works offline.
  • Your videos are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your original is only read, never modified. The compressed copy is saved where you choose, and an existing file is never overwritten.
  • While it works, VidLimit keeps temporary files in its own temporary folder and removes them when finished or canceled.

What we collect

Nothing. VidLimit has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code. It does not track you. The app itself has no network permission, so it cannot make internet connections on its own.

What is stored on your Mac

VidLimit saves a few counters in its own app settings (how many free compressions you have used, how many compressions have succeeded, and whether the rating prompt has been shown). These stay on your Mac and are not sent to us.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles payment, price display, purchase and restore, and the purchase records, under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple ID. Buying or restoring needs an internet connection and is done by macOS, not by VidLimit.

Links to this website

The Help menu has links to the Support and Privacy pages on this website. They open in your default browser only when you click them. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts.

Apple privacy manifest

VidLimit includes an Apple privacy manifest that declares: no tracking, no tracking domains, and no collected data types. It also lists two system-API reasons Apple requires apps to declare: use of app settings storage for the app's own data (the counters above), and reading basic file information (such as size) for files you choose and for VidLimit's own temporary files. These are used only for the app to work and do not leave your Mac.

Children

VidLimit collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact
